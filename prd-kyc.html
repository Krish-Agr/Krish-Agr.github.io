Executive Summary

The KYC Onboarding Agent is an agentic AI system built on a multi-agent LangGraph architecture that autonomously processes Know Your Customer (KYC) applications for retail and SME customers at a Tier-1 Dutch bank. The system orchestrates three specialised agents — document ingestion, identity verification, and AML risk scoring — to reduce average onboarding time from 48 hours to 15 minutes while maintaining full regulatory compliance under GDPR, AML Directive V, and the EU AI Act.

Scope: This PRD covers the full product lifecycle from MVP agent design through risk-committee approval and production pilot deployment. Out of scope: international KYC flows, corporate/institutional onboarding, and integration with legacy core banking systems beyond the defined API contracts.

Problem Statement

Current State

The bank's existing KYC onboarding workflow is entirely manual, requiring compliance analysts to review identity documents, cross-reference sanctions/PEP databases, and produce risk assessments for every application. At 10,000+ daily applications, this creates a structural bottleneck with three compounding problems:

ProblemBusiness ImpactRoot Cause
48-hour average onboarding timeCustomer drop-off estimated at 23% during wait periodSequential manual review with no parallel processing
~45 mins analyst time per application€8.2M annual labour cost for KYC functionNo automation for document extraction or risk scoring
Inconsistent risk decisions3 compliance audit findings in FY23No standardised decision logic; analyst-dependent outcomes
No audit trail standardisationRegulatory risk; manual reconstruction requiredDecisions captured in unstructured notes across systems

Why Previous Automation Attempts Failed

Three prior automation initiatives were rejected by the bank's risk committee before this project. Root cause analysis identified three failure modes: (1) rule-based RPA that couldn't handle document variability; (2) ML models without explainability features, blocked by compliance; (3) a GenAI PoC without grounded retrieval or audit trail, generating hallucinated risk assessments.

Key Insight from Discovery: The fundamental barrier was not technical capability — it was the absence of a product designed to be auditable from the ground up. This PRD treats EU AI Act compliance and explainability as core product requirements, not post-launch additions.

Secondary Metrics (Leading Indicators)

  • Agent confidence score distribution: ≥ 85% of applications resolved with confidence > 0.82
  • HITL escalation rate: ≤ 18% of applications routed to human review
  • Document extraction accuracy: ≥ 97% field-level accuracy on standard identity documents
  • Latency p95: ≤ 90 seconds for end-to-end standard application processing

Anti-Goals

What success does NOT mean: This product is not optimising for maximum automation rate. A 100% automation rate would be a red flag — it would mean the escalation logic is broken. The target HITL rate of 15–18% reflects genuine edge cases that require human judgment. Driving this below 10% without a corresponding reduction in missed risk would indicate the system is over-confidently auto-approving borderline cases.

Personas

Persona 1 — The KYC Analyst (primary user): Maria, 34, processes 60–80 applications per day. Her frustration is not the volume — it's the repetitive extraction work on standard cases. She wants the AI to handle clear-cut cases and surface only the genuinely ambiguous ones, with a clear reason for escalation so she can make a fast, defensible decision. She is sceptical of AI systems that don't explain their reasoning.

Persona 2 — The Compliance Lead (approver): Jan, 52, has seen three AI projects fail compliance review. His concern is not whether the AI is accurate — it's whether he can explain a decision to a regulator three years from now. He needs every automated decision to have a retrievable, human-readable audit trail. He does not need to understand the model; he needs to trust the governance wrapper around it.

User Stories (Priority Backlog)

KYC-001 · Priority: P0 · Estimate: 8 pts
As a KYC analyst, I want the system to automatically extract all required fields from uploaded identity documents, so that I do not manually re-enter data for standard applications.
Acceptance Criteria: ≥97% field-level extraction accuracy on passports, national ID cards, and driving licences from NL, DE, FR, BE. Extraction confidence score displayed per field. Low-confidence fields flagged for analyst review. Processing time ≤ 8 seconds per document.
KYC-002 · Priority: P0 · Estimate: 13 pts
As a compliance officer, I want every automated KYC decision to include a human-readable explanation of the factors considered and the confidence level, so that I can reconstruct the decision logic for regulatory audit.
Acceptance Criteria: Every decision (approve/escalate/reject) includes a structured JSON audit record with: input data hash, agent reasoning chain, confidence scores per factor, regulatory rule references cited, and timestamp. Audit records retrievable by application ID for ≥ 7 years.
KYC-003 · Priority: P0 · Estimate: 13 pts
As a KYC analyst, I want the system to route unclear or high-risk applications to my review queue with a structured summary, so that I can make a fast, informed decision without starting the review from scratch.
Acceptance Criteria: HITL queue displays: application summary, agent confidence score, specific reason for escalation (e.g. "PEP match — confidence 0.61, threshold 0.80"), relevant document excerpts pre-highlighted, and a recommended decision with override option. Analyst review time target: ≤ 8 minutes per escalated case.
KYC-004 · Priority: P1 · Estimate: 8 pts
As a Risk Officer, I want a real-time dashboard showing agent performance metrics and escalation patterns, so that I can detect model drift or unusual decision patterns before they create regulatory exposure.
Acceptance Criteria: Dashboard updates in near real-time (≤ 5 min lag). Shows: hourly application volume, escalation rate (with 7-day trend), confidence score distribution, top escalation reasons, and analyst override rate. Alerting threshold: escalation rate > 25% triggers Slack alert to PM and compliance lead.
KYC-005 · Priority: P1 · Estimate: 5 pts
As a data privacy officer, I want all PII data processed by the AI agents to remain within EU infrastructure and be deleted after the defined retention period, so that we comply with GDPR Article 17 and Article 44.
Acceptance Criteria: All processing on EU-region cloud infrastructure only (no data leaves EEA). Automated deletion job runs nightly for records beyond retention window. Deletion confirmed in audit log. Data residency verified in deployment checklist signed by DPO.

HITL Escalation Logic

Applications are auto-approved when all three agent confidence scores exceed their respective thresholds AND no hard-rule flags are triggered. Applications are escalated to HITL when any of the following conditions apply:

  • Document extraction confidence < 0.90 on any required field
  • Identity verification confidence < 0.85
  • AML risk score ≥ 0.65 (amber) — auto-reject at ≥ 0.90 (red)
  • Any hard rule triggered (e.g. exact PEP name match, sanctions list hit)
  • Document expiry within 30 days
  • Discrepancy between document data and submitted application data > 2 fields
